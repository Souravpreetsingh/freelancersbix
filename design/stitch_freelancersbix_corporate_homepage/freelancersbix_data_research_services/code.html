Verification Standards

Good analysis starts with organized data.

Flawed inputs inevitably generate misleading conclusions. Our quality framework enforces four pillars of scrutiny to ensure analytical validity and executive confidence.

Completeness Systematically isolate missing cells, sample attrition, and truncated records.
Consistency Standardize chronological syntax, naming taxonomies, and numerical conventions.
Structure Reformat tables into normalized relational or flat tabular schema suitable for modeling.

INQUIRIES & CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of data work do you provide? expand_more

We cover data collection, data cleaning, spreadsheet architecture (Excel/Google Sheets), survey data analysis, descriptive and inferential statistical modeling, data visualization, and custom research report synthesis.

Can you clean Excel data? expand_more

Yes. We resolve duplicate rows, split combined text fields, standardize mixed date conventions, identify empty nulls, and ensure the dataset conforms to a clean, usable schema.

Can you analyze survey data? expand_more

Yes. We structure raw survey exports (from platforms like Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, or Google Forms), code open-text entries where applicable, calculate response distributions, perform cross-tabulations, and build visual charts.

Can you create charts and graphs? expand_more

Yes. We develop presentation-ready visualizations including time-series line graphs, comparative bar charts, distribution scatterplots, correlation matrices, and executive dashboard summaries.

Do you provide statistical analysis? expand_more

Yes. We support appropriate quantitative methods—such as central tendency summaries, ANOVA, t-tests, Pearson/Spearman correlations, and regression models—strictly guided by your dataset parameters.

Can you analyze my research dataset? expand_more

Yes. We handle datasets from academic, commercial, and institutional research projects, structuring variables and executing statistical tests aligned with your predefined research hypotheses.

Can you work with Excel files? expand_more

Extensively. We work with all versions of Microsoft Excel (.xlsx, .csv) as well as Google Sheets, architecting complex nested formulas, dynamic range models, and pivot tables.

Do you provide research interpretation? expand_more

Yes. Numbers alone rarely tell a whole story. We explain statistical outputs clearly in plain language within the context of your specific inquiry or business challenge.

Can you guarantee the results of statistical analysis? expand_more

No. Statistical analysis must reflect empirical reality. We guarantee accurate and rigorous methodological execution, but we never alter data to achieve predetermined statistical significance or specific desired outcomes.

Do you provide financial advice? expand_more

No. We provide mathematical data structuring, calculation formulas, and spreadsheet visualization. We do not provide licensed investment advisory, tax strategy, or statutory audit verification.

Can you work with confidential datasets? expand_more

Yes. We execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to data ingest and practice strict data hygiene, anonymization, and secure post-delivery repository purging.

How do I start? expand_more

Simply click "Get a Quote" or contact our team with a summary of your data format, analytical objectives, and deadline. We will review the parameters and provide a tailored scope of work.
