Quality Protocol

How we think about research quality

High quality research is not merely grammatically correct copy; it represents coherent logic, empirical validity, and defensible interpretations grounded in verified literature.

RELEVANCE

Directly addresses the formulated problem statement.

STRUCTURE

Logical, traceable transitions between arguments.

EVIDENCE

All statements verified through high-impact citations.

CLARITY

Unambiguous academic discourse without fluff.
